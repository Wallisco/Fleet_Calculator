How it works

Three things to set up, then the same cycle every week. Figures below are for your fleet as you've set it.

We build the bikes
We run the swap network
We service the bikes
We give you the system to manage it all in

All of it in one price. That last part is the bit that matters.

Set up once
STEP 1
Get your bikes
Choose your fleet size and how you're paying for it. We deliver, register and hand the bikes over ready to ride.
STEP 2
Set your rates in Lighthouse
Lighthouse is your operator portal. Set the weekly rent and the deposit you charge each rider, and it handles the collection from there.
R0 weekly rent · R0 deposit per rider
STEP 3
Put riders on the bikes
Take vetted riders from our marketplace, or bring your own. A filled seat is a bike earning; an empty one is the only thing that really costs you.
0 seats to fill
Then every week
STEP 4
Riders pay you
Paystack collects the rent up front, before the week starts. You hold their deposit against anything unpaid.
R0 collected across your fleet
STEP 5
You pay us R465 a bike
HeroCare covers maintenance, insurance, tracking and recovery. Distance is charged at 60c per km and redeemed from your rider.
R0 out of the money collected
STEP 6
Your payment lands
The balance is paid into your account weekly. No invoicing, no chasing riders, no surprise repair bills.
R0 yours, every week

↻ This cycle repeats every week for as long as the bikes are on the road. Over 3 years that's 156 weeks of collections.
