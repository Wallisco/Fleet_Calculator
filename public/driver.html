Earnings disclaimer. These figures are indicative only. ScootHero uses aggregated, anonymised delivery data from across the industry to show what activity of this kind has typically paid. They are not an offer, promise or guarantee of work, earnings or payment of any amount. ScootHero does not employ delivery riders, does not allocate jobs and does not set or pay these rates. What you actually earn depends on the platform or operator you work for, the jobs you accept, your area, your hours and your own running costs, and may be materially higher or lower than shown.

How these numbers are built. Average per-job rates across delivery activity in June and July 2026 — R58.05 a food delivery and R19.63 an express parcel. Kilometres are worked out from what you spend on petrol, at 24 km a litre. On electric, the weekly rental covers maintenance, tyres, insurance and tracking; you pay only for charging at 62c a kilometre. All figures exclude VAT.
